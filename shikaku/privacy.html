Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Who we are

Shikaku ("the game", Android package com.younessnadi.shikaku) is developed by Youness Nadi, an independent developer based in Italy. Contact: younessnadi17@gmail.com.

What we collect ourselves

Nothing that identifies you. The game has no account and no login. Your progress, settings, streak and gallery are stored only on your device. We do not run our own servers. The game is intended for adults (18+).

Data collected by the services the game uses

The game uses the following third-party services. Depending on your consent and your region, they may collect the data listed here:

  • Google AdMob (Google LLC): shows ads (banner, full-screen ads between levels, optional reward videos). It may collect the device advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID), IP address, approximate location derived from the IP address, device model, OS version, language, app interactions with ads, and diagnostics. Some ads are served by Meta Audience Network (Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd.) through Google's ad mediation, which may collect similar data. See How Google uses information from apps that use its services and Meta's Privacy Policy.
  • Meta SDK (App Events, Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd.): measures app installs and in-game events from advertising (app opened, tutorial completed, levels reached, purchases). It only starts when your consent allows it; otherwise it stays completely off. In the United States it runs with Meta's Limited Data Use option.
  • Google Firebase (Google LLC): Analytics (game events such as a level started or completed, with an app instance identifier, device model, OS version, language and approximate location derived from the IP address), Crashlytics (crash reports with technical details of the device and the error), Remote Config (settings such as how often ads appear, no personal data) and Cloud Messaging (optional news notifications, using a device token and the language you play in). See Privacy and Security in Firebase.
  • Google Play (Google LLC): Play Billing for in-app purchases (purchase history; payment details are handled only by Google, we never see them), Install Referrer (which link led to the install, used for friend challenges), in-app review and in-app updates.

Data types: device or other identifiers (advertising ID, app instance ID); app interactions; crash logs and diagnostics; purchase history; approximate location derived from the IP address by the ad and analytics services. This data is shared for advertising and analytics, is encrypted in transit, and is not linked to a name, email or account (the game has none).

Consent (EU/EEA, UK, Switzerland)

Before any ad is shown and before analytics or the Meta SDK can store data, you are asked for consent through Google's consent form (UMP). Without consent you only see non-personalized ads, Firebase Analytics runs in Google Consent Mode without storing data, and the Meta SDK stays off. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time in the game: Settings → Privacy choices.

US state privacy rights

If you live in a US state with a consumer privacy law, you can opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for targeted advertising through the game's Settings → Privacy choices (when Google shows the US message) or your Android device settings. We do not sell personal information for money. Meta receives data only with its Limited Data Use option.

Notifications

The game only asks for permission to send notifications after you finish your first daily puzzle, and never at launch. If you allow them, a daily reminder is scheduled locally on your device, and you may receive occasional news (for example a new version) through Firebase Cloud Messaging. You can turn them off in the game's Settings or in Android Settings → Apps → Shikaku → Notifications.

Children

The game is intended for adults and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Retention and your rights

Data on your device is deleted when you uninstall the game. Data held by Google and Meta is kept according to their policies (for example Firebase Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months by default). Under the GDPR you can ask for access, correction or deletion of data and you can object to processing. Since we store no personal data on our own servers, write to us and we will help you contact Google or Meta. You can also reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads). No deletion request is needed for the game itself.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date at the top shows the latest version.
