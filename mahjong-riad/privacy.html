English

Mahjong Riad is a single-player puzzle game. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, location or contacts, and you do not need an account.

Data stored on your device. Your progress (level, collection, settings, helpers) is stored only on your phone. The age range you may choose at first launch only sets the default tile size; it never leaves your device and is never shared with advertisers or analytics.

Analytics and crash reports. We use Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config) to understand how levels are played (for example: level started, won or lost, helper used) and to fix crashes. Firebase may collect an app-instance identifier, device model, operating system version, country and app version. This data is not used to identify you.

Advertising. The game shows ads through Google AdMob (with partner networks such as Meta Audience Network through AdMob mediation). Ads may use your device's advertising identifier. Where required (EEA, UK, Switzerland and other regions), we ask for your consent with Google's consent form before personalized ads are shown; you can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy preferences. You can also reset or limit your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

Children. The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Your rights. You can delete all game data by uninstalling the app. For analytics data held by Google, contact us at the address above and we will help with access or deletion requests. Third-party policies: Google (policies.google.com/privacy), Meta (facebook.com/privacy/policy).

Changes. We will update this page if the policy changes.
