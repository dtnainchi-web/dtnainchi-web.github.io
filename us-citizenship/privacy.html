Privacy policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

This policy explains how the app US Citizenship Test 2025 (launcher name "Civics Test", package com.younessnadi.uscitizenship, "the app") handles data. Developer and controller: Youness Nadi (Google Play developer "MaxQuizz"), Italy. Contact: dtnainchi@gmail.com.

Independent study app. Not affiliated with USCIS or the U.S. government. The civics questions are U.S. government public-domain text. Official information: uscis.gov/citizenship.

No account, no personal data from you

The app has no registration or login. It never asks for your name, e-mail, phone number, immigration status or any document. We do not run any server that receives your study data.

Data stored only on your phone

Your state, ZIP code and congressional district (typed by you, used only to show the right senators, governor and representative), your 65/20 choice, language, answers, saved questions, mistakes, daily-lesson progress, day streak, practice-test scores and reminder settings stay in the app's local storage on your device. They are never sent to us. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them. The app does not use your location (no location permission).

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, reminders are scheduled locally on your phone. You can turn them off in the app (Settings) or in your phone settings.

Text-to-speech

"Listen" uses your phone's own text-to-speech voice. The text is processed by the speech engine installed on your phone (for example Google Speech Services) under that engine's terms; the app sends nothing to us.

Ads: Google AdMob and Meta Audience Network

The app is free and shows ads through Google AdMob (Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited). Some ads may come from Meta Audience Network (Meta Platforms, Inc. / Meta Platforms Ireland Limited) through AdMob mediation. To serve, measure and protect ads these companies may process your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from the IP address, device and app information, and ad interactions. Learn more: How Google uses data, Meta Privacy Policy.

Consent (Google User Messaging Platform)

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland the app shows Google's consent form before any ad is requested. If you consent (or where consent is not required), ads may be personalized and the measurement tools below are on. If you refuse, or if consent cannot be obtained, you only see non-personalized ads, Meta App Events stays off and Firebase Analytics runs in Consent Mode with analytics and ad storage denied. You can change your choice at any time in the app: Settings › Privacy options; the change applies immediately. On Android you can also reset or delete your advertising ID in the phone settings.

Meta App Events

Only with consent (or where consent is not required), the app sends Meta a few anonymous usage events (app activation, daily lesson completed, practice test passed) with the advertising ID, to measure ad campaigns.

Firebase (Google)

Google Analytics for Firebase: usage statistics (screens and actions such as finishing the daily lesson or a practice test, app language, the state you selected, device model, approximate country, app-instance ID); data retention 14 months. Firebase Crashlytics: crash reports (device state and app state at the time of the crash), only in release builds; kept 90 days. Firebase Remote Config: app settings such as ad frequency. More: Privacy and Security in Firebase.

Files downloaded from the web

At start the app downloads small public files from GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.): the current officials (officials.json), content fixes (fixes.json) and the list of our other apps (apps.json). GitHub receives your IP address as for any web page; the app sends nothing else.

Legal bases, retention, transfers

Personalized ads and measurement are based on your consent where the law requires it; non-personalized ads and crash reports are based on legitimate interest (keeping the app free and stable). Google and Meta may transfer data outside your country under their own safeguards (such as Standard Contractual Clauses). Local data stays on your device until you delete it.

Your rights

You can ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability, and withdraw consent at any time (Settings › Privacy options). Because we hold no personal data on our side, most requests are handled by deleting the app's data on your phone; for data held by Google or Meta, use their tools or write to us and we will help. California residents: we do not sell personal information for money; you can opt out of personalized ads through Privacy options or your device's ad settings. You may complain to your data protection authority.

Children

The app is meant for adults (18 and over) preparing for the naturalization interview and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18; a parent or guardian can email dtnainchi@gmail.com to have any such data deleted.

Changes

Changes will be posted on this page with a new date.

Política de privacidad

Última actualización: 6 de noviembre de 2026

Esta política explica cómo la aplicación US Citizenship Test 2025 ("Civics Test", com.younessnadi.uscitizenship) trata los datos. Desarrollador y responsable: Youness Nadi (desarrollador "MaxQuizz" en Google Play), Italia. Contacto: dtnainchi@gmail.com.

Aplicación de estudio independiente. No está afiliada a USCIS ni al gobierno de los EE. UU. Las preguntas de educación cívica son texto de dominio público del gobierno de los EE. UU. Información oficial: uscis.gov/es/ciudadania.

Sin cuenta

La aplicación no tiene registro ni inicio de sesión. Nunca le pide su nombre, correo, teléfono, situación migratoria ni documentos. No tenemos ningún servidor que reciba sus datos de estudio.

Datos guardados solo en su teléfono

Su estado, código ZIP y distrito (que usted escribe, solo para mostrar sus senadores, gobernador y representante), su opción 65/20, idioma, respuestas, preguntas guardadas, errores, progreso, racha de días, resultados de exámenes de práctica y recordatorios se guardan solo en su dispositivo. No se nos envían. Se borran al desinstalar la aplicación o borrar sus datos. La aplicación no usa su ubicación.

Notificaciones y voz

Los recordatorios, si los activa, se programan localmente en su teléfono. "Escuchar" usa la voz de texto a voz de su propio teléfono.

Anuncios: Google AdMob y Meta Audience Network

La aplicación es gratuita y muestra anuncios de Google AdMob; algunos pueden venir de Meta Audience Network mediante la mediación de AdMob. Estas empresas pueden tratar el ID de publicidad, la dirección IP, la ubicación aproximada, datos del dispositivo y las interacciones con los anuncios para mostrarlos, medirlos y protegerlos.

Consentimiento

En el Espacio Económico Europeo, el Reino Unido y Suiza, la aplicación muestra el formulario de consentimiento de Google antes de pedir anuncios. Con su consentimiento (o donde no se requiere) los anuncios pueden ser personalizados y se activan Meta App Events y Firebase Analytics. Si lo rechaza, solo verá anuncios no personalizados, Meta queda desactivado y Firebase funciona en modo de consentimiento denegado. Puede cambiar su elección en Ajustes › Opciones de privacidad; el cambio se aplica de inmediato.

Firebase y Meta App Events

Google Analytics for Firebase (estadísticas de uso, idioma, estado elegido, modelo del dispositivo, país aproximado; 14 meses), Firebase Crashlytics (informes de fallos; 90 días) y Firebase Remote Config (ajustes de la aplicación). Solo con consentimiento, la aplicación envía a Meta algunos eventos anónimos (lección diaria completada, examen de práctica aprobado).

Archivos de internet

Al iniciar, la aplicación descarga de GitHub Pages pequeños archivos públicos (funcionarios actuales, correcciones, lista de nuestras aplicaciones). GitHub ve su dirección IP como en cualquier página web.

Sus derechos

Puede pedir acceso, corrección, eliminación, oposición y retirar su consentimiento en cualquier momento escribiendo a dtnainchi@gmail.com. La aplicación está dirigida a adultos (18 años o más) y no a niños. No recopilamos a sabiendas datos de menores de 18 años; un padre, madre o tutor puede escribir a dtnainchi@gmail.com para que eliminemos esos datos.
