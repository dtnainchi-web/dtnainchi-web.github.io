1. What the app is

US Citizenship Test 2025 ("Civics Test") is an independent study app. It is not affiliated with USCIS, the Department of Homeland Security or the U.S. government, and it is not legal advice. It helps you study the 2025 civics test questions published by USCIS (U.S. government public-domain text). Always check official information at uscis.gov/citizenship and uscis.gov/citizenship/testupdates.

2. Accuracy

We work carefully to keep the questions, answers, translations and current officials correct, but we cannot guarantee that everything is complete or up to date at all times. Answers about officials change after elections and appointments: at your interview you must give the name of the official serving at that time. Translations and explanations are study aids; in the interview the officer asks in English (or in your language only if you qualify for a USCIS language exception, such as 50/20, 55/15 or 65/20, and bring an interpreter). We do not guarantee that you will pass any test.
