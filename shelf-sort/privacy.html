Who we are

Shelf Sort: Goods Match ("the game") is developed by Youness Nadi, an independent developer based in Italy. Contact: younessnadi17@gmail.com.

What we collect

We do not ask for your name, email, or any account. The game has no login. Your progress (levels, coins, stars, settings) is stored only on your device.

Advertising (Unity Ads)

The game shows ads provided by Unity Ads (Unity Technologies). To show and measure ads, Unity may collect device information such as the advertising identifier (IDFA, only if you allow tracking), IP address, device model, OS version, and ad interactions. See Unity's Privacy Policy.

On iOS you are asked for permission before any tracking (App Tracking Transparency). In the EU/EEA/UK you are asked for consent through a consent form; you can change your choice at any time from the game's Settings.

Notifications

If you allow them, the game schedules reminder notifications locally on your device (for example when a daily reward is ready). No data is sent to a server. You can turn them off in the game's Settings or in iOS Settings.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask for access, correction or deletion of data. Since we do not store personal data on our servers, deleting the game removes all local data. For data held by Unity, use the links in Unity's policy or contact us and we will help.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date at the top shows the latest version.
