FAQ

How do I play? Drag items between shelves. Put three identical items on the same shelf to clear them. Clear every shelf before time runs out.

Is my progress saved? Yes, on your device. Deleting the app deletes your progress.

How do I turn off notifications? Open Settings in the game, or iOS Settings → Shelf Sort → Notifications.

How do I change ad consent? Open Settings in the game and tap Privacy options.
