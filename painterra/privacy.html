English

This policy explains what data the Android game Painterra ("the app") processes. The data controller is Youness Nadi, an independent developer based in Italy. Contact: younessnadi17@gmail.com.

No account, no sale of data

The app has no login and no user accounts. Your progress is stored on your device. We do not sell your personal data.

Services in the app and the data they process

  • Google AdMob (ads) and the Google User Messaging Platform (consent form): advertising ID, IP-derived approximate location, device information and ad interactions. Google Privacy Policy, How Google uses data from apps.
  • Meta Audience Network (ads served through AdMob): advertising ID, device information and ad interactions. Meta Privacy Policy.
  • Meta App Events (Facebook SDK, facebook-core): advertising ID, app events (for example app install, level completed), install referrer and purchase events, used to measure advertising. Only active when you allow it in the consent form where consent is required. Meta Privacy Policy.
  • Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics and Remote Config (Google): app usage events, app instance ID, device information, crash reports and diagnostics, and settings fetched from the server. Privacy and Security in Firebase.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google): a registration token and the app version, used only to send update notifications (topics by app language). Tapping one opens this game's Google Play page. You can turn notifications off in Android settings.
  • Google Play Install Referrer: how the app was found on Google Play, used to measure advertising.
  • Google Play Billing and Unity IAP: purchases are processed by Google Play. Unity IAP passes the purchase to Google Play on your device; we receive the order confirmation to unlock the item, not your payment details. Google Payments Privacy Notice, Unity Player Privacy Policy.
  • Android Auto Backup: your save file (progress and settings) may be backed up to your own Google account by Android. It is not sent to us.
  • Local notifications: optional reminders created on your device. No data leaves the device for them. You can turn them off in the app or in Android settings.

Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland)

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app shows Google's consent form before personalised ads, Meta App Events and analytics for advertising are used. You can change your choice at any time in the app: Settings → Privacy preferences. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads or Google → Ads).

Without consent: if you do not consent to analytics, Firebase Analytics runs in Google Consent Mode with analytics storage denied. The app then sends Google only cookieless pings without identifiers (no app instance ID, no advertising ID), which Google uses for aggregated, modelled statistics. No analytics profile is created for you. Crash reports (Crashlytics) are not affected by this choice.

GDPR information (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

Controller: Youness Nadi, independent developer based in Italy, younessnadi17@gmail.com.

Purposes and legal bases (Art. 6 GDPR):

  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)): analytics with Firebase Analytics, personalised ads (Google AdMob, Meta Audience Network) and ad measurement with Meta App Events. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, analytics storage, personalised ads and Meta App Events are used only after you agree in the consent form (Meta App Events only if you also allow Meta among the ad partners). Without that consent, Firebase Analytics only receives the cookieless, identifier-free pings of Google Consent Mode described below.
  • Contract (Art. 6(1)(b)): providing the game you installed, saving your progress, delivering and restoring purchases, and loading game settings (Remote Config).
  • Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)): crash reports and diagnostics (Crashlytics) to fix errors, security and fraud prevention, non-personalised ads that keep the game free, and update notifications (you can turn them off in Android settings). Our interest is a stable, safe and free game; you can object at any time.
  • Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)): keeping purchase records as required by tax and accounting law.

You are not required to provide any data. Analytics, personalised ads and Meta App Events are optional; without the data needed to run the game (for example the device identifiers in ad requests and purchase confirmations), ads and purchases cannot work. We do not make decisions with legal or similarly significant effects based solely on automated processing.

Retention: progress and settings stay on your device until you uninstall the app or clear its data. Analytics events are kept for up to 14 months, crash reports for up to 90 days, and Firebase installation IDs and notification tokens until they become inactive or you uninstall the app. Google and Meta keep ad data under their own retention policies. Purchase records are kept for as long as tax law requires (in Italy up to 10 years).

Your rights: you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and the right to object to processing based on legitimate interest. You can withdraw your consent at any time in the app (Settings → Privacy preferences); this does not affect processing done before. To use your rights, e-mail us; we answer within one month. Because the app has no accounts, we may ask for details that help us find your data (for example the date and order number of a purchase).

Complaints: you have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the EU country where you live or work, or with the Italian authority, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali (garanteprivacy.it).

International transfers: Google (Google LLC) and Meta (Meta Platforms, Inc.) may process data in the United States. These transfers rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (and its UK and Swiss extensions), under which both companies are certified, and/or on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

US state privacy rights

If you live in a US state with a consumer privacy law (for example California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Utah or Texas), this section applies to you. We do not sell personal information for money. The advertising partners in the app (Google AdMob, Meta Audience Network and Meta App Events) receive identifiers such as the advertising ID and app usage data (for example ad interactions and app events) for cross-context behavioural advertising; under the CCPA/CPRA and other state laws this may count as "sharing" or "targeted advertising". When you opt out, the game shows non-personalised ads and applies Meta's Limited Data Use (LDU) to the Meta SDK and Meta Audience Network.
